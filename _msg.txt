Read the NEWEST bot message, not the oldest one in the window

"Check quota now" kept reporting "no reply from the bot to the quota
command" while the answer ("Active : 50047") was already visible in the
chat. _last_bot_message iterated its window in reverse, but read_messages
returns newest-first - both Telethon's iter_messages and Pyrogram's
get_chat_history do - so it consistently picked the OLDEST message it had
fetched. Anchoring and reply-matching were then comparing against stale
history, which is why the earlier timing fixes looked ineffective.

Also adds GET /api/otpbot/chat_tail: the last few messages in the target
chat exactly as the automation sees them (id, direction, text). This is
what made the bug visible - the run said "no reply", the chat tail showed
the reply sitting there two ids above - and it turns any future "it says it
did not answer" into something checkable instead of guessable.

realistic interleaved history.
