Deliver button presses: callback_query was missing from allowed_updates

Every inline button in the OTP panel did nothing. There was no error and no
log line, which is what made it confusing: start_polling was called with
allowed_updates=["message"], so Telegram dropped callback_query updates
server-side and the handler was never reached at all. The handler, the
keyboards and the callback payloads were all correct - the updates simply
never arrived.

Added a test that drives the real handler for each button (interval,
cleanup, service, skip, per-country interval, preset, clear queue, refresh
countries) plus the delivery config itself, and verified it by
reintroducing the bug: the allowed_updates assertion fails with
["message"] and passes with callback_query included.

Also covered the cases that only show up in a live chat: an unauthorised
user tapping a button, a button tapped long after its country was removed
(buttons live forever in chat history), and malformed payloads - none of
which may raise inside the handler.

682 passing.
