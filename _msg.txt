Fix infinite recursion in _sleep that broke every real OTP-bot run

"Start" failed with a bare "maximum recursion depth exceeded" and nothing
else to go on. Two separate mistakes combined to produce that:

1. The bulk rename of asyncio.sleep -> _sleep (introduced so tests could
   skip the real delays) also rewrote the body of _sleep itself, so it
   called itself forever. Every test monkeypatches _sleep, so the entire
   suite passed while nothing worked in production - the exact blind spot
   a mocked-out helper creates.

2. start_automation caught RuntimeError to report "the bot rejected your
   file". RecursionError subclasses RuntimeError, so a hard crash was
   presented to the owner as a bot rejection, pointing the investigation
   at @PBDxbot instead of at our own code.

Fixes: _sleep calls asyncio.sleep again; bot rejections now raise a
dedicated AddRejected exception instead of a broad built-in; unexpected
errors report their exception type and log a full traceback, so the next
crash is diagnosable from the container logs rather than from one
context-free sentence.

Regression tests: one exercises the REAL _sleep (reloading a pristine
module, since the autouse fixture patches it away) and was verified to
fail with RecursionError when the bug is reintroduced; another asserts a
genuine exception is reported as a crash, not as a bot rejection.

Also surfaced by the debugging: the Telegram userbot is currently not
linked (status linked=False), so /tglogin is still needed before a real
run can succeed - that is a separate, pre-existing condition, now
reported accurately instead of being masked.

598 tests (was 596), smoke unaffected.
