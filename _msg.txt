Probe a dead provider less often the longer it stays dead

The live gateway does not have a bad key, it returns 502 - every time.
Each attempt costs ~30s (its client retries three times) before the
working provider is tried, and a fixed cooldown meant paying that once
per window, forever.

A provider that fails AGAIN after its cooldown expired is not blipping,
it is down: the cooldown now doubles per consecutive failure, capped at
two hours. One success resets it completely, so a provider that comes
back is trusted again immediately rather than being punished for its
outage.

Auth failures start from an hour rather than two minutes for the same
reason - a wrong key cannot fix itself the way a rate limit can. Neither
is a blacklist: pasting a working key takes effect on the next message.
